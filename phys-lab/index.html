产品设计说明

给团队看的一页 PRD：我们在为谁解决什么问题、凭什么有效、内容从哪来、下一步做什么。

定位用户核心循环学习科学赛制与目标内容体系与知识图谱教练端指标路线图

一、定位：不是题库，是「有教练的训练场」

面向 G9–G11 备考 BPhO 与 PhysicsBowl 的中国学生，把「刷什么、刷多少、错了怎么办」三件事从学生和家长手里接过来，让每一次刷题都有明确的目的。

市面上的现状是：BPhO 官方题库和 PhysicsBowl 历年真题都是公开 PDF，学生「有题可刷」，但普遍卡在三处——不知道自己弱在哪个专题、做错的题过几天就忘、Section 1 / 限时选择两种完全不同的节奏没有分开训练。师说物竞的差异点是把竞赛教练的判断（先练什么、哪些题值得重做、考前怎么分配时间）产品化为一套自动运转的学习循环，教练只在关键节点介入。

一句话

「每天 25 分钟，系统告诉你今天该做的 8 道题，并保证做错的题会在你即将忘记时回来。」

二、用户与场景

G9 · 起步物理刚入门，先赢一次

目标：PhysicsBowl Div1 + BPhO Intermediate Challenge。核心需求是「不被难题吓退」。产品给他：低门槛速答、即时反馈、可见的进步曲线。

G10 · 主力两赛并进，冲 BPhO 银 / 金

目标：R1 Silver→Gold、PhysicsBowl Div1/2 前 10%。核心需求是「专题补漏 + 节奏切换」。产品给他：雷达图、间隔复习、双模式训练。

G11 · 冲刺申请季前最后一次拿硬奖

目标：R1 Top Gold、进 Round 2、PhysicsBowl 全球榜。核心需求是「高效率 + 模考校准」。产品给他：整卷模考、选题策略训练、预测分数带。

次要用户：竞赛教练（看班级弱项、指派题组、审核错因）与家长（每周一封进度简报，不看题只看趋势）。产品同时提供中英文界面：中文面向国内学生与家长，英文面向国际学校教师与海外学生。

三、核心循环（每天 25 分钟）

  1. 到期复习（约 3 题）：间隔重复算法推回来的错题 / 低信心题。这是每天的第一件事，因为记忆提取在临忘点效果最好。
  2. 新题推送（约 4 题）：从掌握度最低的两个专题中交错抽题，难度锚定在「当前正确率 60–75%」的区间。
  3. 节奏训练（1 组）：BPhO 季是 1 道 Section 1 风格的数值题（限 4 分钟）；PhysicsBowl 季是 5 题 / 5.5 分钟的速答组。
  4. 作答前标信心 → 作答后标错因：每题两次元认知动作，共约 3 秒，是整个产品最重要的数据来源。
  5. 收尾反馈：不给「你真棒」，给「今天电路专题正确率从 55% 到 70%，主要错因从『概念』变成『计算』——说明概念通了，下一步练准确度」。

四、学习科学机制（为什么有效）

检索练习 Retrieval practice

解答默认折叠，必须先作答（或明确「跳过」）才能看解析。对比重读，提取式学习在一周后的保留率高出 50% 以上。

Roediger & Karpicke 2006
间隔重复 Spaced repetition

SM-2 简化版：答对且自信 → 间隔 ×2.2；答对但不确定 → ×1.3；答错 → 次日回归并进错题本。间隔序列 1 → 3 → 7 → 16 → 35 天。

Cepeda et al. 2006 · Wozniak SM-2
交错练习 Interleaving

「智能推送」不按章节顺序，而是把 2–3 个专题混排，迫使学生先判断「这是哪类题」再解——这正是竞赛与课本练习最大的差别。

Rohrer & Taylor 2007
元认知校准 Metacognitive calibration

作答前四档信心（很确定 / 大概 / 猜的 / 完全不会）。数据页画出「信心 × 正确率」校准图，让「自以为会」这一类最危险的漏洞可见。

Dunlosky & Metcalfe 2009
错因归类 Error taxonomy

五类错因：概念不清 / 建模错误 / 数学与计算 / 审题遗漏 / 时间不够。不同错因对应不同处方——概念错回专题讲义，计算错练精度，时间错练节奏。

Ericsson 刻意练习 · Bjork 合意困难
提示阶梯 Faded scaffolding

三级提示：思路方向 → 关键公式 → 完整解答。低年级默认可用提示 1–2，高年级默认关闭，降低认知负荷的同时不替学生思考。

Sweller 认知负荷 · Renkl 渐隐样例
目标梯度 & 进度可见

倒计时、连续打卡、掌握度条、预测分数带——让「离 Gold 还差多少」成为一个具体数字，而不是焦虑。

Locke & Latham 目标设定
过程性反馈 Growth-oriented feedback

反馈只描述行为与变化（「这周你把 4 道概念错改成了对」），不评价能力。竞赛周期长、挫败多，反馈语言直接决定留存。

Dweck · Hattie & Timperley 2007

六、内容体系

  • 题源分层：① 官方真题（BPhO R1 2002–2024、Round 0、PhysicsBowl 历年）→ ② 官方题库按专题切分的分题（备考资源已盘点 32 个 PDF）→ ③ 自研变式题（同一物理模型换情境，用于间隔复习避免「记答案」）。
  • 题目元数据：所属赛事、专题、知识点、难度（L1 课本 / L2 竞赛基础 / L3 竞赛进阶）、目标用时、错因高频标签、前置知识点。这是推送算法的全部依据。
  • 录入流程：图片版 PDF → OCR → 教练审核答案与解析 → 标注元数据 → 中英双语解析。首期目标 600 题（BPhO 350 / PhysicsBowl 250），当前原型内置 44 题双语示例。
  • 解析规范：先写「这题在考什么模型」一句话，再写步骤；每步只出现一个物理判断；结尾给「常见错法」。

知识体系：十专题 × 62 个知识点 × 三层

「知识图谱」页把两场比赛的大纲合并成一张图。专题沿用 BPhO 官方题库的十个分类；每个知识点标注三件事：层级（核心 = 两赛共同主干；拓展 = BPhO R1 / PB D2 常考但课本不深讲；进阶 = R2 与 D2 第 41–50 题的大学内容）、首次出现的比赛层级（IPC → SPC → R1 → R2；D1 / D2）、对应课程位置（A-level 年级、AP Physics 1/2/C、国内必修/选修）。题库中每道题都关联到一个知识点，因此「掌握度」可以从专题下钻到知识点；学生也可以对每个知识点自评「未学 / 在学 / 已掌握」。

备考资源与考试须知

七、教练端与家长端（MVP 之后）

教练看三张表：班级专题热力图、本周高频错因、每个学生的「信心 × 正确率」校准偏差。教练可以给单个学生指派一组题并附一句话，学生在「今日」页看到。家长每周日收到一封 200 字简报：本周做题量、连续天数、最进步的专题、下周重点——不含题目、不含排名。

八、北极星与护栏指标

  • 北极星：每周完成 ≥4 天日循环的学生比例（目标 60%）。
  • 学习效果：到期复习题的二次正确率（目标 ≥75%）；模考预测分与真实分差（目标 ±8 分）。
  • 护栏：单日做题量上限 30 题（防止刷量代替思考）；连续 3 天信心「很确定」却答错比例 >30% 时触发教练提醒。

1. Positioning: a coached training ground, not a question bank

For Chinese students in Grades 9–11 preparing for BPhO and PhysicsBowl. We take three decisions off the student's and parents' hands — what to practise, how much, and what to do after a mistake — so every problem solved has a purpose.

Both contests publish their past papers as free PDFs, so students have plenty to practise. They get stuck in three places: not knowing which topic is weak, forgetting a corrected mistake within days, and never separating the two very different rhythms of BPhO Section 1 and a timed multiple-choice paper. Our difference is turning the coach's judgement — what to practise first, which mistakes deserve a redo, how to allocate exam time — into a self-running learning loop, with the coach stepping in only at key moments.

In one sentence

"Twenty-five minutes a day: the system tells you today's eight problems and makes sure every mistake comes back right before you would forget it."

2. Users and scenarios

G9 · StarterNew to physics — win once first

Targets: PhysicsBowl Div1 + BPhO Intermediate Challenge. Core need: not to be scared off by hard problems. We give them low-threshold rapid items, instant feedback and a visible progress curve.

G10 · CoreBoth contests, aiming at BPhO Silver / Gold

Targets: R1 Silver→Gold, PhysicsBowl Div1/2 top 10%. Core need: plug topic gaps and switch rhythms. We give them the radar, spaced review and dual-mode training.

G11 · SprintLast hard award before applications

Targets: R1 Top Gold, a Round 2 invitation, PhysicsBowl global list. Core need: efficiency and calibrated mocks. We give them full-paper mocks, question-selection drills and a predicted award band.

Secondary users: coaches (class weak spots, assigned sets, error-tag review) and parents (a weekly progress note — trends, never questions). The interface is bilingual: Chinese for domestic students and parents, English for international-school teachers and overseas students.

3. The core loop (25 minutes a day)

  1. Due review (~3 items): mistakes and low-confidence items returned by the spacing algorithm. Always first — retrieval works best right before forgetting.
  2. New items (~4): interleaved from the two weakest topics, pitched at the 60–75% accuracy band.
  3. Rhythm block (1 set): in BPhO season one Section-1-style numerical item (4-minute cap); in PhysicsBowl season a 5-question / 5.5-minute rapid set.
  4. Confidence before, error tag after: two metacognitive taps per item, about three seconds — the most valuable data the product collects.
  5. Closing feedback: never "well done", always "circuits went from 55% to 70% today and your main error moved from concept to arithmetic — the concept has landed, now train precision".

4. Learning-science mechanisms (why it works)

Retrieval practice

Solutions stay folded until the student answers (or explicitly skips). Retrieval beats re-reading by 50%+ on one-week retention.

Roediger & Karpicke 2006
Spaced repetition

Simplified SM-2: correct and confident → interval ×2.2; correct but unsure → ×1.3; wrong → back tomorrow and into the notebook. Sequence 1 → 3 → 7 → 16 → 35 days.

Cepeda et al. 2006 · Wozniak SM-2
Interleaving

Smart push mixes 2–3 topics rather than following chapter order, forcing the student to identify the problem type before solving — exactly what separates contests from textbook exercises.

Rohrer & Taylor 2007
Metacognitive calibration

Four confidence levels before answering. The analytics page plots confidence against accuracy, exposing the most dangerous gap: "I thought I knew that".

Dunlosky & Metcalfe 2009
Error taxonomy

Five tags: concept / modelling / maths / misread / time. Each maps to a prescription — concept errors go back to the topic notes, arithmetic errors train precision, time errors train pacing.

Ericsson deliberate practice · Bjork desirable difficulties
Faded scaffolding

Three hint levels: approach → key formula → full solution. On by default for younger students, off for seniors — lower cognitive load without thinking for them.

Sweller · Renkl faded worked examples
Goal gradient & visible progress

Countdowns, streaks, mastery bars and a predicted band turn "how far from Gold?" into a number rather than anxiety.

Locke & Latham goal setting
Growth-oriented feedback

6. Content system

  • Three tiers of sources: ① official papers (BPhO R1 2002–2024, Round 0, PhysicsBowl by year) → ② the official topic-sorted question bank (32 PDFs inventoried in Resources) → ③ in-house variants (same physical model, new context, for spaced review without answer-memorising).
  • Item metadata: contest, topic, knowledge point, level (L1 textbook / L2 contest basic / L3 contest advanced), target time, frequent error tags, prerequisites — everything the push algorithm uses.
  • Pipeline: scanned PDF → OCR → coach checks answer and solution → metadata → bilingual solution. First target 600 items (BPhO 350 / PhysicsBowl 250); the prototype carries 44 bilingual samples.
  • Solution style: one sentence naming the model, then steps with one physics decision each, ending with the common wrong approach.

Knowledge system: 10 topics × 62 knowledge points × 3 tiers

The Knowledge Map merges both syllabi into one picture. Topics follow the ten official BPhO bank categories; each knowledge point carries its tier (core = shared backbone; extension = common in BPhO R1 / PB D2 but shallow in textbooks; advanced = university material seen only in R2 and D2 Q41–50), the first contest level where it appears (IPC → SPC → R1 → R2; D1 / D2) and its curriculum location (A-level year, AP Physics 1/2/C, Chinese compulsory/elective). Every bank item is linked to a knowledge point, so mastery drills down from topic to point, and students can self-rate each point as not started / learning / mastered.

Prep resources and exam notices

The Prep Resources page gathers a year-by-year index of past papers (BPhO R1/R2/SPC/IPC and PhysicsBowl 2014–2025 official PDFs), the official entry points for both contests (BPhO papers and banks for every round, AAPT PhysicsBowl papers and solutions), China-region registration and score-line pages, question-by-question topic maps (all 50 questions of PhysicsBowl 2022 and 2025, every part of BPhO 2024 R1, and the 2002–2013 official bank counted by topic) and a short list of books and sites, each marked free / paid and by level. The Exam Notices page puts formats, dates, registration, room rules, scoring and awards, a pre-exam checklist and FAQs for both contests in one place; China-region details follow the ASDAN notice of the year.

Sources: the official BPhO formula sheet and bank categories, bpho.org.uk competition pages, the AAPT PhysicsBowl FAQ and 2022 / 2025 papers, the AAPT 2025 statistics report, bpho.net.cn historical score lines, and China-region PhysicsBowl notices for 2025–2026.

7. Coach and parent views (after MVP)

Coaches see three tables: a class topic heat-map, this week's frequent error tags, and each student's confidence-accuracy calibration gap. A coach can assign a set to one student with a one-line note, shown on the student's Today page. Parents receive a 200-word note every Sunday: volume, streak, most-improved topic, next week's focus — no questions, no rankings.

8. North-star and guard-rail metrics

  • North star: share of students completing the daily loop on ≥4 days a week (target 60%).
  • Learning effect: second-attempt accuracy on due reviews (target ≥75%); gap between predicted and actual mock score (target ±8).
  • Guard rails: 30 items a day maximum (volume must not replace thinking); a coach alert when "very sure" answers are wrong >30% for three days running.
